## The pipeline

1. **Ingest** — load and normalise sources.
2. **Chunk** — split into retrievable units.
3. **Embed & index** — store vectors with metadata.
4. **Retrieve** — find the most relevant chunks.
5. **Generate** — answer grounded in retrieved context.

## Chunking is a modelling decision

For structured data, one entity per chunk (a "card") beats fixed-length windows because every retrieved unit is complete.
