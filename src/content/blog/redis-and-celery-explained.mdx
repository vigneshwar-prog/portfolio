## Why background workers

Requests should be fast. Anything slow — SSH sessions, reports, emails — belongs in a worker.

## The moving parts

- **Broker** (Redis): holds queued tasks.
- **Workers** (Celery): pull tasks and run them.
- **Result backend**: optional storage of results.
